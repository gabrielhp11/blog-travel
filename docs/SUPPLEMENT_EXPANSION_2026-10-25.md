## Buying details that affect decisions

- HeroSana's label says one gummy daily, while the sales page discusses two and preselects a monthly subscription. The guide follows the label, explains 30 versus 15 days arithmetically, and distinguishes one-time purchase from recurring deliveries.
- SlimSana prints 286% NRV for 100 µg chromium; GlucoSana prints 250% for that amount. The guide preserves and flags the source discrepancy rather than interpreting it as superior quality.
- Several German pages advertise €9 single-bottle shipping while the initial checkout displays €0 before a complete address. Brazil was blocked. No final shipping guarantee is made.
- Gluconite's one-bottle checkout displayed $69 plus $12.97 shipping, total $81.97 before a full address. Its refund policy describes 180 days from shipment, returning all bottles, customer-paid postage and prior support confirmation. The policy references ClickBank while this affiliate offer uses Digistore24; the guide asks readers to check their order-specific terms.
- Insulin Herb's sales FAQ says US only despite international boilerplate in its general policy. Its shipping statements differ for a single bottle. Sixty capsules last 20 or 30 days under the seller's different directions; the guide gives arithmetic, not dosage advice.
