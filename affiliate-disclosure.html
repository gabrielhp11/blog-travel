Affiliate disclosure & editorial standards

Affiliate links: Some links on PokiSky are affiliate links. If you purchase through one, we may receive a commission from the seller. This does not add a fee to your order.

How we describe products

We aim to identify the product, summarize the information available from its seller, and make clear when a statement comes from the seller rather than from independent testing. Unless a page explicitly documents a first-hand evaluation, treat it as an overview of published product information—not a personal test or a clinical assessment.

We do not publish ratings, customer quotations, ingredient quantities, prices, guarantees, or performance results as our own unless we can substantiate them. Seller claims and product details can change; check the linked seller page and product label before deciding.

Health products

Supplement pages are for general information and are not medical advice. A supplement is not a substitute for diagnosis or treatment. Review the current label and consult a qualified health professional about suitability, interactions, pregnancy, existing conditions, or medication use.
