GLP-1 Plus: read the full label

The label defines a serving as two capsules, with 30 servings per container. Amounts below are per label serving.

Chromium (from chromium picolinate)
200 mcg
Gymnema sylvestre extract (25% gymnemic acids)
400 mg
Dihydroberberine (GlucoVantage)
200 mg
CaloriBurn GP
160 mg
Cinnamomum cassia extract (30% polyphenols)
100 mg

Gymnema is identified as a leaf extract and cassia as a bark extract. The seller identifies CaloriBurn GP as a spice extract. The label also prints an ‘equivalent to 1,000 mg berberine’ statement alongside 200 mg dihydroberberine; this guide does not establish clinical or medicine equivalence. Other ingredients include hydroxypropyl methylcellulose, microcrystalline cellulose, calcium stearate and silica.

The list above is an overview, not a replacement for the full label. Label amounts are product facts, not personal dosage recommendations. Read the current package and discuss suitability with a qualified professional.
