Separate quantity from the sales presentation

The seller describes two to three capsules per day while advertising a 30-day bottle. The arithmetic is 60 ÷ 2 = 30 days and 60 ÷ 3 = 20 days. This explains the inconsistency; it is not a personal dosage recommendation. Ask for the exact current directions and full excipient list before buying.

Medical questions come before package size

Do not follow the sales-page suggestion to combine this supplement with metformin without a qualified clinician’s assessment. The supplier’s disclaimer advises professional review for medication, medical conditions, pregnancy or nursing. This guide does not establish that the product treats diabetes or replaces prescribed care.
