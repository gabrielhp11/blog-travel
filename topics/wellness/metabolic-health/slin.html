One bottle, three bottles and the linked six-bottle bundle differ

The official three-bottle artwork below illustrates packaging quantity. It is not the six-bottle offer opened by our affiliate link. At four capsules per serving, 120 capsules contain 30 servings; that arithmetic is not an instruction to use the product for 30 days. A format marketed around high-carbohydrate meals does not establish fat loss, muscle gain or a substitute for a balanced diet.

Warnings and suitability

Published usage wording differs between the page’s directions and meal-related FAQ. Do not combine those passages into a larger dosing schedule. Have a qualified health professional review the complete current label and suitability, especially if you use medication, have a medical condition, are pregnant or nursing. Resolve the Gymnema discrepancy first.
