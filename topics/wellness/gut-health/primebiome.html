Label

Separate the probiotic entry from the botanical blend

The wrapper lists one gummy per serving and thirty servings. Bacillus coagulans has its own CFU entry; the remaining named ingredients are grouped into a 430 mg blend. A species name alone does not identify a particular clinical-study strain.

Label entryPublished detail
Serving and pack1 gummy; 30 servings
Bacillus coagulans500 million CFU listed; strain code not stated
Proprietary blend430 mg total
Blend ingredientsChicory-root inulin, babchi, fennel, fenugreek, ginger, lemon balm, lion’s mane, slippery elm and dandelion
Sugar and carbohydrate entries1 g total sugars; less than 1 g added sugars; 2 g carbohydrate
Other ingredient examplesGlucose syrup, sucrose, apple pectin, citric acid, vegetable oil and carnauba wax

CFU is a count of viable organisms, whereas mg measures mass. Neither the 430 mg mixture nor a photograph of gummies supplies the missing strain identifier or each botanical amount. The reviewed panel does not state clearly whether its CFU count is guaranteed through expiry; ask the seller before comparing probiotic products.

Delivery: confirm the actual destination quote

The general table says US delivery is free with an estimated five to seven working days, while the smaller pack card adds shipping. Confirm the exact package and eligible address. A delivery estimate and an initial country selector do not guarantee arrival or international eligibility.

Returns: check the deadline and the procedure

The return policy allows sixty days from delivery, requires all bottles and the packing slip, and excludes customer return postage. Its suggestion to try the product for three months conflicts with that shorter period. Keep the order-specific deadline and contact support early rather than assuming the trial suggestion extends it.

Gummy format and skin claims need separate judgments

The ingredient list is useful for comparing formulations, but it does not prove the seller’s skin-renewal or weight claims. PokiSky has not tested this product. Review the full botanical mixture, sugars, medicines and suitability with a qualified professional where needed; a gummy is still a supplement rather than a substitute for skin or digestive care.

Questions before choosing

Is 430 mg the probiotic amount?

No. It is the total botanical and fibre blend. Bacillus coagulans is listed separately at 500 million CFU.

Does the Brazil checkout rejection establish US costs?

No. An eligible US address and the final checkout total still need to be checked.
