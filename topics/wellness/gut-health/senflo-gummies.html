Pack-day labels are not advice for continuous senna use

The seller advertises one, three and six bottles as 15-, 45- and 90-day supplies. Those are commercial package descriptions, not instructions to take a stimulant laxative for those lengths of time. The same sales page mixes daily-use language with occasional-use wording. This guide follows neither as a personal dosing plan.

Warnings and suitability

NHS guidance describes senna as a stimulant laxative and advises against using it longer than seven days unless a doctor or pharmacist recommends it. Seek professional advice if symptoms persist or frequent relief is needed. That general guidance does not confirm the strength or suitability of these gummies. Read the full current label with a qualified professional, particularly for pregnancy, nursing, medication or a medical condition. Do not replace an assessment with “natural” or “gentle” sales language.

Returns: read the conditions behind the guarantee

The supplier policy allows a refund request within 60 days of purchase, asks customers to contact support@senflogummies.com, and says refunds go to the original payment method, usually taking 5–10 business days. It lists a Lakeland, Florida return address but does not clearly establish who pays return postage. Obtain instructions before sending a parcel; a refund promise does not verify ingredient strength or product results.
