Digestive is a category, not one mechanism or use pattern

SenFlo names senna, which NHS guidance identifies as a stimulant laxative. Integrative Digestive Formula instead presents botanical, mushroom and enzyme blends. A broad reference to digestive comfort does not establish the same purpose, strength or suitability for either product.

NHS guidance advises against senna use beyond seven days unless a doctor or pharmacist recommends it. That general advice does not verify these gummies' strength or provide a dosing schedule. It does show why the seller's 15-, 45- or 90-day package descriptions must not be read as recommendations for continuous use.

Both products leave different quantitative questions open

For SenFlo, the missing full current Supplement Facts is the first obstacle. The front-bottle artwork and the word senna do not establish the amount per gummy or a complete warning list. Asking for the panel is more useful than trying to infer strength from a commercial supply-day claim.

Integrative Digestive Formula provides three blend totals, but not the individual botanical amounts or enzyme activity units. A milligram total of enzymes does not supply the missing activity information. The more detailed panel therefore improves some comparisons without resolving every clinical or ingredient question.

Keep the two delivery markets separate

The SenFlo checkout recorded US$68.90 for one bottle including initial shipping, despite free-shipping language on the sales page. The Integrative price is a product-only reference from a UK-listed order route. Those figures are not equivalent landed prices and do not justify declaring one product better value.

Before ordering, resolve the label gaps and request the final total for each eligible address. The registered guides record different return periods: 60 days for SenFlo and 90 days for Integrative, with postage details needing confirmation. Persistent symptoms or frequent laxative needs belong in a conversation with a qualified professional.

Editorial conclusion

These are different digestive proposals with different information gaps. Request SenFlo's quantitative panel first; ask for individual amounts and enzyme activity detail for Integrative. Neither the category label nor the pack-day marketing supports substitution or combined use.

Before deciding

  1. Request the full current SenFlo panel and senna amount.
  2. Do not treat package days as a laxative-use course.
  3. Ask for enzyme activity units and individual blend amounts where relevant.
  4. Compare eligible destinations and full delivered totals separately.
