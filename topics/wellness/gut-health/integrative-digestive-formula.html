A closer look at the label.

The label defines a serving as one capsule, with 60 servings per container. Amounts below are per label serving.

Zinc (as OptiZinc zinc methionine)
7.5 mg
Chromium (as polynicotinate)
100 mcg
Integrative Asian Digestive Blend
450 mg
Organically Grown Digestive Mushroom Blend
60 mg
Digestive Enzymes Support Blend
52 mg

The botanical blend lists pomegranate seed, lesser galangal, pepper fruit, piper longum, cassia bark, cardamom, traditional Chinese botanical extracts, tangerine fruit, deglycyrrhizinated licorice, ginger root, henon bamboo and sacred lotus seed. The mushroom blend lists cordyceps, hericium, maitake and poria. The enzyme blend lists amylase, protease, phytase, invertase and lipase. Individual ingredient amounts and enzyme activity units are not disclosed in the displayed label. Other ingredients include hypromellose, water, microcrystalline cellulose and magnesium stearate.

The list above is an overview, not a replacement for the full label. Label amounts are product facts, not personal dosage recommendations. Read the current package and discuss suitability with a qualified professional.
