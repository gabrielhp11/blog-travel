Source: supplier formula artwork

250 mg of extract supplies 125 mg of trans-resveratrol

The supplier’s Supplement Facts artwork lists a one-capsule serving and 30 servings per bottle. It identifies 250 mg of Japanese knotweed root extract standardized to 50% trans-resveratrol. The resulting 125 mg is a constituent of that extract, not an additional 125 mg to add to it.

The artwork also names magnesium stearate, lemon grass powder, white rice flour and cellulose. Its layout places some of these alongside the main ingredient; confirm the complete excipient list on the bottle you receive. The sales phrase “one ingredient” should not be read as an absence of capsule materials or other ingredients.

The supplier describes one capsule daily. This is a description of its directions, not an individual dosage recommendation. Discuss suitability and interactions with a qualified healthcare professional, especially if you take medicines, have a medical condition, are pregnant or nursing.

Compare the same quantity when reading other labels

A listing for 250 mg of botanical extract and a listing for 125 mg of trans-resveratrol describe different measures. For a useful comparison, record the amount of the named compound, the serving size and any other ingredients separately.

Standardization states a composition target. It does not establish that the finished AGE+ product improves health, slows aging or works better than another supplement. Research links on the seller’s page concern resveratrol; they do not amount to a clinical trial of this bottle.

What the 60-day return promise requires

The refund policy permits a return request within 60 days of purchase. It directs buyers to contact Digistore24 Customer Support and obtain return instructions before mailing the product.

For a change-of-mind return, the customer pays return shipping. Original shipping is generally non-refundable unless the item is damaged, defective or incorrect. The policy states no restocking fee and refunds to the original payment method; eligibility and processing follow the order instructions. This does not make a purchase cost-free or automatically refundable.

Does AGE+ reverse aging?

This guide does not establish that claim. The seller markets a healthy-aging supplement, and ingredient research does not prove an anti-aging outcome for the finished product.

Can I order it to Brazil or Europe?

The published policy says US shipping only. Brazil was rejected in the checkout we inspected; international availability is not established.

Is 250 mg the trans-resveratrol amount?

No. The supplier artwork describes 250 mg of Japanese knotweed extract standardized to 50%, providing 125 mg trans-resveratrol per capsule.
