Compare the named compound rather than the extract headline

AGE+'s 250 mg extract is standardized to supply 125 mg trans-resveratrol. Those are nested quantities: adding them would count the constituent twice. The Mitochondrial panel separately lists 25 mg trans-resveratrol within a wider formula and defines a two-capsule serving.

The differing stated amounts identify a composition difference. They do not prove that more trans-resveratrol produces a better health outcome, or that either formula extends life. A supplier's research links about an ingredient are not automatically clinical evidence for the finished bottle.

A broader formula also broadens the comparison

Advanced Mitochondrial Formula names CoQ10, D-ribose, niacinamide, magnesium and several botanical or other compounds. AGE+ centres on one extract, while still listing capsule materials and excipients. A focused formula is not the same as literally having no other ingredients.

For readers already using supplements, comparing overlap across the complete labels is more useful than choosing by ingredient count. Do not create a stack from a shared healthy-ageing theme. Suitability and medication interactions should be reviewed with a qualified health professional using the actual current panels.

The apparent entry-price similarity hides market differences

The recorded single-bottle figures are close, but AGE+'s supplier policy lists US-only delivery and an advertised US$9.99 charge for the smaller packs. The initial checkout instead showed zero shipping. The Mitochondrial guide records a UK-selected three-bottle order. None is a matching final quote for the same address.

The registered AGE+ policy describes a 60-day request period and customer-paid change-of-mind return postage. Mitochondrial's inspected checkout describes 90 days and customer-paid return postage. Confirm the governing order policy and original-shipping treatment. A longer guarantee is a purchasing condition, not evidence of longer life or better energy.

Editorial conclusion

AGE+ is a focused extract-based label; Advanced Mitochondrial Formula is a broader combination with a smaller declared trans-resveratrol amount per label serving. Compare overlap and eligibility before price. The available material does not identify a clinical winner for longevity or fatigue.

Before deciding

  1. Do not add 125 mg trans-resveratrol to its parent 250 mg extract.
  2. Compare complete formulas and existing supplement overlap.
  3. Confirm that a suitable buying route serves your country.
  4. Obtain the final shipping quote and order-specific return terms.
