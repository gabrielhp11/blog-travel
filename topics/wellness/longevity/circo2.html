Before you decide

Questions about CircO2.

Is CircO2 a physical product?

Yes. It is a boxed food supplement containing dissolving tablets, delivered to your address. Each box is labelled as containing 30 tablets.

Is this a substitute for prescribed treatment?

No. Do not change prescribed treatment based on a product sales page. Discuss the ingredients and possible interactions with a qualified health professional.

It states amounts for vitamins C and B12, magnesium and the total proprietary blend. Amounts for the four individual blend ingredients are not provided.
