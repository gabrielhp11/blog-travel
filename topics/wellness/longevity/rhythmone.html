Label

Keep the wrapper’s caution beside its serving directions

The supplier wrapper states a two-millilitre serving and thirty servings per bottle. It groups the ingredients into one 820 mg proprietary blend. The same wrapper says that product safety has not been determined; that statement must remain visible rather than being replaced with a general natural-product reassurance.

Label entryPublished detail
Bottle and serving60 ml; serving 2 ml; 30 servings
Proprietary blend820 mg total
Mushroom ingredientsTurkey tail, reishi, lion’s mane, chaga, cordyceps and king’s trumpet
Other blend materialsMCT oil powder, cacao powder and shilajit extract standardized to 40% fulvic acid
Other ingredientsWater, glycerin, natural flavors, potassium sorbate and sodium benzoate
Safety statementThe wrapper says product safety has not been determined

The wrapper gives a daily two-millilitre direction and also mentions an optional second serving. That changes the arithmetic from thirty to fifteen days per bottle, but is not an instruction from PokiSky. Pause for a qualified professional’s review and clarification of the safety statement rather than deciding a dose from the sales narrative.

Delivery: confirm the actual destination quote

The shipping page says shipping is included, while the smaller sales-page packs add $9.99 and its footer says international charges apply. Obtain the actual quote and timing for the destination; no complete address was entered for this guide.

Returns: check the deadline and the procedure

The sales presentation advertises a 365-day guarantee, but the linked shipping-and-returns page does not set out a detailed refund period or full claim procedure. Ask support for the applicable deadline, opened-bottle rules, return postage and written instructions before payment.

A longevity story is not product-level proof

The presentation discusses circadian timing and mushroom research. Ingredient references do not establish that this exact mixture produces its advertised cognitive or aging outcomes. Individual amounts are undisclosed, and the explicit safety wording remains unresolved. PokiSky has not tested the product and does not recommend an extra serving or substitution for medical care.

Questions before choosing

Is each mushroom present at 820 mg?

Does this guide recommend taking a second serving?
