Buying BrainAMP: questions to settle first

Is the Alpha-GPC entry 215 mg of pure Alpha-GPC?

No. The displayed label identifies 215 mg of a 70% Alpha-GPC ingredient per two capsules. Compare the ingredient form as well as the amount, and read the complete label before discussing suitability.

Is BrainAMP simply a caffeine capsule?

The label lists paraxanthine, alongside several other compounds and extracts. Do not assume that this composition is equivalent to coffee or that it is suitable for everyone.
