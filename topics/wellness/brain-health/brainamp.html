Understand the product before the promise.

BrainAMP is a multi-ingredient supplement from Advanced Bionutritionals. It combines amino-related compounds, botanical extracts and carotenoids. A capsule format avoids preparing a drink, but suitability still depends on the ingredients and your circumstances.

A closer look at the label.

The label defines a serving as two capsules, with 30 servings per container. Amounts below are per label serving.

350 mg
Alpha-GPC 70% (GeniusPure)
215 mg
Paraxanthine (enfinity)
200 mg
Coffee fruit extract (CognatiQ)
100 mg
Marigold flower extract (Lutemax 2020)
100 mg
Salidroside (SalidroPure)
20 mg

The marigold extract specifies 20 mg lutein and 4 mg zeaxanthin. The 215 mg Alpha-GPC entry describes a 70% ingredient, not 215 mg of pure Alpha-GPC. Other ingredients include the cellulose capsule, cellulose, silica, bamboo extract and calcium stearate.

The list above is an overview, not a replacement for the full label. Label amounts are product facts, not personal dosage recommendations. Read the current package and discuss suitability with a qualified professional.

Before you decide

Questions about BrainAMP.

Is the product information in English?

The seller page, label and inspected order form are in English. This is a physical supplement delivered to your address.

Is BrainAMP simply a caffeine capsule?

The label lists paraxanthine, alongside several other compounds and extracts. Do not assume that this composition is equivalent to coffee or that it is suitable for everyone.
