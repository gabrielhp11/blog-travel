Label

The CFU claim has a specific time point

The wrapper lists a one-tablet serving and 30 servings per bottle. It distinguishes a shared probiotic blend from a separate bacterial entry.

Label entryPublished detail
Inulin powder100 mg
Proprietary probiotic blend20 mg; 3.5 billion CFU at manufacture
Blend organismsLactobacillus paracasei, Lactobacillus reuteri and Bifidobacterium lactis BL-04
Streptococcus salivarius20 mg; no separate CFU amount shown

The 3.5 billion CFU statement applies at manufacture, not a verified quantity at expiry. The FAQ says one tablet daily, while the wrapper allows one tablet once or twice daily. Thirty tablets mean 30 days at one daily or 15 days at two daily, as arithmetic only—not dosing advice. Ask the seller and a qualified professional to reconcile the directions.

Delivery: confirm the actual destination quote

The supplier shipping table lists free US shipping and an estimated 5–7 working days. It lists $15.95 and 10–15 working days for Canada, UK/Ireland and Australia/New Zealand. These are supplier estimates, not an address-specific quote or a promise that every destination is eligible. Confirm the actual order total and destination before paying.

Returns: check the deadline and the procedure

The written policy gives 180 days from delivery, requires contacting support and returning all bottles, empty or otherwise, plus the packing slip. Return shipping is paid by the buyer. Its suggestion to try the product for six months should not cause you to miss the exact 180-day deadline.

A probiotic panel does not prove a memory outcome

No independent trial or memory-treatment effect is established for the finished product here. The seller’s claims about removing sugar buildup from neurons are not independently validated by this review. Discuss the exact probiotic formula, current medicines and individual suitability with a qualified health professional before use.

Questions before choosing

Are 3.5 billion CFU guaranteed at the end of shelf life?

The wrapper specifically says at manufacture. It does not establish the same viable count at expiry.

Will one bottle necessarily last a month?

There are 30 tablets. The directions differ; at two tablets daily the arithmetic is 15 days, not 30.
