Label

The probiotic line and its footnote disagree

The published wrapper defines one capsule as a serving and lists thirty servings. Its probiotic row says fifteen billion CFU, while the related footnote says thirty billion live CFU at manufacture. These figures should not be silently reconciled or presented as an expiry-date guarantee.

The panel names Lactobacillus paracasei, buchneri, brevis and hilgardii, without full strain identifiers. Ask the seller which CFU figure applies to the actual bottle and when it is guaranteed. The shared-facility allergen warning also deserves attention; natural ingredient wording is not an allergy clearance.

These are dated sales-page figures. They are not a complete destination quote or a promise that advertised stock counters represent actual scarcity. The general policy says shipping is included, which conflicts with smaller-pack surcharges on the offer page.

Delivery: confirm the actual destination quote

The shipping page separates preparation and carrier delivery and gives longer estimates for international orders. Confirm the eligible destination, full timing, taxes and the conflicting shipping charge in the actual checkout.

Returns: check the deadline and the procedure

The detailed policy states a 365-day refund period and customer-paid return postage. The sales copy also says a customer can keep the supply. Ask whether the exact order requires a return, when the period starts and which costs are excluded; do not describe the headline as unconditional reimbursement of every cost.

The label does not establish a cure for tinnitus

The sales presentation attributes tinnitus to a specific microplastic and neurotransmitter mechanism and describes dramatic recoveries. This guide has not verified those assertions or independently tested the formula. Do not change prescribed care or delay assessment because of the presentation. A qualified hearing or medical professional can assess symptoms and discuss the complete ingredient list.

Questions before choosing

Does the label confirm fifteen or thirty billion CFU?

It contains both figures in different places. Request a corrected, current label rather than choosing one.

Does the guide confirm that MindSilence treats tinnitus?

No. It documents the supplier’s label and purchase terms; it does not establish treatment effectiveness.
