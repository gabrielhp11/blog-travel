Ingredient form matters more than a headline count

Both panels permit a form-aware comparison. BrainAMP lists 215 mg of a 70% Alpha-GPC ingredient per two capsules. Memory Formula lists 250 mg glycerophosphocholine supplying 50% alpha-GPC per two tablets. The ingredient weights and percentages must stay together; neither number is the pure compound weight by itself.

Memory Formula also declares a 150 mg phosphatidylserine complex from soy lecithin, 120 mg bacopa extract and 80 mg ginkgo, among other entries. It explicitly states Contains Soy. Both formulas name coffee-fruit ingredients, but the wider compositions differ. A shared ingredient family does not establish equivalent absorption or finished-product effects.

Separate focus marketing from a memory assessment

BrainAMP declares paraxanthine alongside L-tyrosine and other ingredients. That is a concrete distinction to discuss with a qualified professional in the context of medicines and other products. We do not turn it into a claim about alertness, calm, productivity or onset time.

Advanced Memory Formula now has a readable quantitative panel in this review, rather than only an eleven-ingredient narrative. That improves label comparison, but it is still not a clinical test of memory improvement. Seller ingredient citations and customer quotations do not establish a head-to-head advantage. Persistent or concerning memory changes deserve qualified assessment.

A store price is not automatically the affiliate order price

The manufacturer store for Memory Formula displays single-payment prices, including a one-bottle offer. Our existing guide links to a different seller route. Keep that distinction visible before comparing it with BrainAMP's dated Digistore24 package figures, and confirm that the formula version matches.

Ask both sellers to confirm that the quantitative panel matches the exact current order, along with the quantity, eligible address and delivered total. BrainAMP’s registered guide describes a 90-day return period with customer-paid postage. Confirm the Memory Formula order rules separately, and review the soy warning with a qualified professional rather than transferring assumptions between formulas.

Editorial conclusion
