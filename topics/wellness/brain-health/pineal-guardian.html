The single-bottle sales card adds $9.99 shipping, while the multi-bottle cards advertise free shipping. Confirm the destination-specific quote, dispatch estimate and any optional ShipSelect service in the final order.

Returns: check the deadline and the procedure

The sales page advertises a 365-day money-back guarantee. Obtain the order-specific start date, return procedure, treatment of empty bottles and responsibility for postage in writing. A guarantee is not evidence of safety or effectiveness.

Keep memory concerns separate from a supplement sales story

Questions before choosing

Is this the same formula as every older Pineal Guardian offer?

The reviewed label and current artwork say Pineal Guardian X. Confirm the exact version on your order rather than transferring an older formula’s claims.

Does 1,202 mg mean that much of each botanical?

No. It is the total of the entire proprietary blend. Separate component quantities are not given.
