The autoship option advertises twenty percent savings and free US shipping, with cancellation or pausing. It is a recurring commitment, not a one-off three-bottle discount. Review the first charge, later schedule and cancellation deadline in the selected plan.

Delivery: confirm the actual destination quote

The shipping policy lists domestic fees from $8.95 for ordinary orders and a seven-to-ten-business-day estimate. International rates and duties differ. Free-shipping autoship wording does not establish free delivery to every country or for a separate Digistore24 offer.

Returns: check the deadline and the procedure

The store policy covers purchases within ninety days, requires bottle returns and gives an estimated three-to-five-business-day refund process after receipt. Ask which original shipping costs and return-postage rules apply to the selected order. These store terms should not be assumed for a different affiliate checkout.

Published ingredients are not a personal cognitive assessment

The supplier uses strong focus and memory claims. This guide documents formulation and order differences, not a measured improvement or a diagnosis. PokiSky has not tested Focus IQ. Discuss the entire formula, fish allergy, medicines and suitability with a qualified professional before use when relevant.

Questions before choosing

Does vegetable cellulose mean fish-free?

No. The store explicitly discloses fish and the panel contains EPA and DHA from an omega-3 ingredient.

Are the store and a separate checkout necessarily the same offer?

No. This guide compares the official store options. Verify the exact package, billing schedule and written terms if another sales page leads to a different checkout.
