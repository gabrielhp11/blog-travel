Supplier narrative · morning format

The morning formula needs its own complete label

The seller lists tongkat ali extract, L-citrulline malate, ginkgo biloba, zinc, vitamins B6, B9, B12, D3 and K1, selenium, copper, iodine, saw palmetto, piperine and citrus bioflavonoids in its morning narrative. This list is not a verified complete Supplement Facts panel: the morning amounts, excipients and total serving composition were not established in the material inspected.

The seller describes one capsule with water after waking. Treat that as its usage description, not an individual recommendation. Ask for a readable current label before comparing doses or combining it with a multivitamin or another supplement.

The product is marketed particularly to adult men over 40. That audience positioning does not establish suitability, safety or a need for supplementation for every reader in that age group.

What the published evening panel actually lists

The supplier’s linked Evening Complex label proof describes one capsule per serving, 30 servings per bottle and five active ingredients:

  • Zinc as zinc picolinate: 15 mg.
  • L-citrulline malate: 400 mg.
  • Horny goat weed, leaf and stem, standardized to 40% icariins: 100 mg.
  • Tongkat ali root extract: 40 mg.
  • Piperine from black pepper fruit extract: 2 mg.

It lists a methylcellulose capsule as the other ingredient and directs one capsule daily in the evening. The file is a label-production proof, not a laboratory certificate or confirmation of the formulation currently shipped. Do not add these amounts to an assumed morning formula; ask the supplier to reconcile the images, narrative and both current labels first.

Resolve the label differences before relying on the claims

The distinction between a morning and evening routine is the seller’s product format. This guide does not establish better sleep, increased testosterone, improved circulation, sexual performance or any other clinical outcome for the finished products. Customer videos and manufacturing badges on the sales page do not supply that evidence, and PokiSky has not independently verified their certification claims.

The published evening warning advises consultation with a healthcare professional before use for people taking medication, with medical conditions, or who are pregnant or nursing. Bring both complete labels to that discussion so overlapping vitamins, minerals and botanicals can be considered. Do not replace prescribed treatment or choose a supplement to diagnose or treat symptoms.

If a complete current label is a requirement for your purchase decision, pause the purchase until the supplier provides it. The missing morning quantities are a meaningful limitation of this guide.
