Suitability and returns

Make the health check first.

New or persistent urinary symptoms need clinical assessment, not a supplement trial. NHS guidance advises urgent help if you cannot pass urine. Blood in urine also needs urgent assessment. Discuss medicines, existing conditions and the complete formula with a qualified professional before use.

The seller and order form advertise a 90-day return policy, including opened bottles, with return postage paid by the buyer. The sales text says original shipping is refundable. Confirm the current deadline, return address and refund handling before ordering; return postage can still be a cost.
