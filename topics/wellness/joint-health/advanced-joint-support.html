Suitability and returns

Make the health check first.

The label says the product is not intended for people under 18. Discuss the complete formula with a qualified health professional if you take medicines, have a condition, or are pregnant or nursing. Do not use seller testimonials to replace an assessment of ongoing joint pain.

The order form advertises a 90-day return policy, including opened bottles, with return postage paid by the buyer. Confirm the return address, deadline and treatment of shipping charges before ordering.
