An amino-acid blend and an HMB formula answer different label questions

Advanced Amino Formula identifies eight amino acids and a combined 5,000 mg blend. The total does not reveal how much leucine or any other individual component is present. Advanced Muscle Plus separately declares dileucine and calcium HMB. These are different ingredient statements, not quantities to place on one scale and rank by total milligrams.

The calcium line on the Muscle Plus panel comes from its calcium HMB ingredient. Adding that calcium number to the HMB amount would double-count part of the same ingredient. Neither a larger combined weight nor more tablets establishes better strength or recovery.

Read the vitamin D caution before choosing a pack

The displayed Muscle Plus label lists 200 µg vitamin D per six-tablet serving. NHS public guidance warns adults against taking more than 100 µg daily unless a doctor advises otherwise. The published entry is therefore a reason for individual medical review, not a routine dose recommendation or a printing error we can assume away.

Do not reduce tablets or create a new schedule to work around that warning based on this article. Ask a qualified clinician to assess the complete formula and all other sources of vitamin D. The absence of the same vitamin entry from the reviewed Amino Formula panel does not establish that the alternative is suitable for everyone.

Price comparisons need matching quantities and delivery

The recorded one-bottle prices differ, but both products use 30 label servings per container, with different formulas. This supports a package-cost comparison only after suitability is settled. A lower cost per label serving is not a cost per unit of muscle gained.

The registered guides also open different packs and record different country-level shipping examples. Ask for matching quotes to the same address. Both guides describe a 90-day return period with customer-paid postage; the Muscle Plus materials also disagree about treatment of original shipping. Editorial conclusion

Amino Formula is a combined amino-acid blend with undisclosed individual amounts. Muscle Plus separately lists dileucine, HMB and a vitamin D entry requiring medical review. The first decision is whether the formulation is appropriate, not which pack appears cheaper.

Before deciding

  1. Read the 200 µg vitamin D entry with a qualified clinician.
  2. Do not add calcium as an extra amount of HMB.
  3. Request individual amino-acid amounts if they determine your comparison.
  4. Compare matching pack sizes, destinations and refund conditions.
