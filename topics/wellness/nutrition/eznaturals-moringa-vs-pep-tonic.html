Do not convert the capsule into an imaginary scoop equivalent

Moringa's three entries describe leaf powder and two extracts. The 10:1 and 20:1 ratios are supplier ingredient descriptions, not evidence that a capsule supplies a particular serving of whole vegetables. Adding extrapolated raw-leaf equivalents would hide assumptions that have not been verified here.

Pep Tonic's 10 g scoop includes fibre and several separately stated compounds alongside two blends. Its green-drink presentation is therefore not equivalent to a single-plant capsule. Neither format establishes nutritional equivalence to a balanced diet or a finished-product energy benefit.

Compare practical preferences with the full composition

The capsule may be simpler to carry, while the powder requires measuring and mixing. Those are format observations, not claims of better adherence or absorption. Preferences about flavour, sweeteners and capsule materials should be checked against the actual ingredients rather than inferred from the product category.

Moringa's label includes a facility statement about common allergens. Pep Tonic includes flavouring, acids and stevia as well as its botanical and other compounds. Ask a qualified professional about the full formula, especially with medication, pregnancy, nursing or an existing condition. Do not assume that plant-based means suitable for everyone.

Supply labels and delivery claims need a second look

Moringa's bottle has 60 one-capsule servings, while the commercial 30-day description reflects a different assumed frequency. Its published one-to-two-times-daily wording explains that arithmetic; it is not a personal schedule we recommend. Pep Tonic has 30 labelled scoops, which is a different quantity measure.

The registered offers target different markets. The Moringa guide records a US checkout example with tax; the Pep Tonic guide discusses UK availability and possible destination costs. Compare current delivered totals only after confirming that both routes serve your address. Their 60- and 90-day guarantee descriptions also have different conditions and postage uncertainties.

Editorial conclusion

Moringa offers a more focused botanical composition in a capsule. Pep Tonic provides a broader mixed-drink formula with fibre and undisclosed within-blend amounts. The right comparison concerns the complete ingredients, preferred format and available buying route; neither is established as a replacement for food.

Before deciding

  1. Keep powder, extract ratios and scoop weight as separate measures.
  2. Read the full ingredient and allergen information.
  3. Do not infer a personal schedule from commercial supply days.
  4. Check whether both offers serve your address before comparing delivered cost.
