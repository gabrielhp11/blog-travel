Delivery: confirm the actual destination quote

The supplier shipping table lists free US shipping and an estimated 5–7 working days. It lists $15.95 and 10–15 working days for Canada, UK/Ireland and Australia/New Zealand. These are supplier estimates, not an address-specific quote or a promise that every destination is eligible. Confirm the actual order total and destination before paying.

Returns: check the deadline and the procedure

The written refund policy starts its 60-day period on delivery. It asks you to email support and return all bottles, empty or otherwise, with identifying order details and the packing slip. The buyer pays return shipping. Its suggestion to use the product for three months exceeds that deadline; obtain clarification before ordering and do not wait past the return window.

Maintain professional dental care

No independent product trial, gum-disease treatment or oxygenation outcome is established here. The product is a dietary supplement, not a replacement for dental examination or prescribed care. Review the full ingredient list and your current medicines with a qualified health professional before use.

Questions before choosing

Does the 120 mg blend tell me the amount of shilajith?

No. The panel lists only the combined amount, not a separate quantity for each component.

Is it suitable for every age as the FAQ suggests?

That broad claim conflicts with the wrapper, which says not for people under 18 and not for pregnant or nursing users.
