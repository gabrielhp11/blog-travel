Delivery: confirm the actual destination quote

The seller advertises free shipping and estimates 3–6 business days for US orders and 9–28 days internationally. These are supplier statements; confirm the eligible destination and full quote in the actual order.

Returns: check the deadline and the procedure

The seller advertises 180 days and says empty bottles can qualify. Another section frames the guarantee around using the product for 180 days. Ask support to confirm the actual start date, when a request must be made, what must be returned and who pays postage before ordering.

Do not treat blanket safety statements as verification

The supplier says there are no side effects and uses FDA facility language. This guide does not verify those statements or establish that the formula treats neuropathy. Facility registration is not FDA approval of the supplement. Ask a qualified health professional to review the full current label and continue prescribed care.

Questions before choosing

Is the complete formula confirmed by the plant pictures?

No. Illustrations and a plant list do not establish amounts, other ingredients or a current full label.

Are the daily directions consistent?

No. The headline says one daily capsule and the FAQ describes twice-daily use. Obtain a clear current label and professional advice before use.
