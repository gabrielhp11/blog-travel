One strip is a serving, not one tin

The published Supplement Facts lists 1 oral strip (210 mg) and 30 servings per tin. The 1 mg melatonin amount is per strip. The plant extracts are separate ingredients, not additional melatonin. A dissolving format does not itself establish faster action or better absorption.
