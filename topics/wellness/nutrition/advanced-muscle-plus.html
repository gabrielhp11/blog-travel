Suitability and returns

Make the health check first.

The NHS advises adults against taking more than 100 mcg vitamin D daily unless a doctor has recommended a different amount. This product’s displayed serving supplies twice that amount. Obtain individual medical advice before use, including a review of other supplements. We do not recommend a substitute dose or taking fewer tablets to work around this issue.

The order form advertises a 90-day return policy, including opened bottles, with return postage paid by the buyer. The seller’s longer guarantee text excludes shipping from refunds, while the order form says full refund. Confirm whether original shipping is refundable.
