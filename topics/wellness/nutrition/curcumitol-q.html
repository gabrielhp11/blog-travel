A closer look at the label.

The label defines a serving as one capsule, with 60 servings per container. Amounts below are per label serving.

Turmeric root ingredient (Curcumin BioBDMC 30)
377 mg
Quercetin (from Japonica Pagoda)
50 mg

The label gives standardised curcuminoid proportions: curcumin I 52%, curcumin II 12% and curcumin III (BDMC) 30%. These percentages describe the ingredient specification, not percentages of the whole capsule or measures of clinical effectiveness. Other ingredients are silicon dioxide and rice hull powder; the capsule contains hypromellose and purified water.

The list above is an overview, not a replacement for the full label. Label amounts are product facts, not personal dosage recommendations. Read the current package and discuss suitability with a qualified professional.
