Label

The probiotic amount is in mg, not a CFU guarantee

One capsule is the labelled serving, with thirty servings per container. The panel splits the formula into a 350 mg herbal mixture and a separate 50 mg probiotic mixture. It does not supply an amount for each botanical or a viable-organism count for the probiotic blend.

Label entryPublished detail
Herbal blend350 mg total: mimosa pudica, cranberry extract, bearberry and granular berberine HCl extract
Probiotic blend50 mg total; CFU count not stated
Listed strain identifiersL. crispatus LCr86; L. acidophilus LA85; L. plantarum Lp05; L. gasseri LG08; L. casei LC89
Serving and bottle1 capsule; 30 servings
Capsule and other ingredientsGelatin, brown rice flour, magnesium stearate, olive oil and silicon dioxide

The cranberry specification and the berberine extract description do not reveal individual delivered amounts. Similarly, 50 mg cannot be converted into CFU without additional information. Request the current complete panel and storage requirements before treating this as equivalent to another probiotic product.

Delivery: confirm the actual destination quote

The general table says US shipping is free, but the two-bottle card adds $9.99. Ask which amount applies to your exact order, including tax and destination eligibility. No complete address quote was obtained for this guide.

Returns: check the deadline and the procedure

The policy’s introduction starts sixty days at the order date, while its first step starts at delivery. It also suggests three months of use. Ask support to resolve both timing conflicts in writing. The policy requires all bottles and the packing slip, with return postage paid by the customer.

Urinary symptoms should not be reduced to a marketing mechanism

The seller presents a urinary-microbiome explanation, but the reviewed label and sales narrative do not establish that this product treats bladder leaks or infection. Do not use the blend name as a diagnosis. Discuss persistent or new urinary symptoms and the full formula with a qualified clinician; PokiSky has not independently tested the supplement.

Questions before choosing

Does 50 mg mean 50 million CFU?

No. Mass and viable-organism count are different measurements; the reviewed panel does not give that conversion.

Is the product a urinary infection treatment?

This guide does not establish treatment effectiveness. Urinary symptoms require appropriate professional assessment.
