Delivery: confirm the actual destination quote

The supplier shipping table lists free US shipping and an estimated 5–7 working days. It lists $15.95 and 10–15 working days for Canada, UK/Ireland and Australia/New Zealand. These are supplier estimates, not an address-specific quote or a promise that every destination is eligible. Confirm the actual order total and destination before paying.

Returns: check the deadline and the procedure

The sales presentation refers to 60 days from purchase, but the dedicated written policy uses 60 days from shipment. It requires returning all bottles and completing the return form, with postage paid by the buyer. Contact support before returning anything and obtain the order-specific deadline in writing.

Nerve-support marketing does not replace medical care

This guide does not verify that Nervogen Pro treats neuropathy, pain or another condition. The named-botanical list is insufficient to judge an individual’s suitability or interactions. Have a qualified health professional review the exact current panel before use and continue prescribed care.
