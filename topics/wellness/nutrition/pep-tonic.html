The label also lists coenzyme Q10 (100 mg), beta-NMN (300 mg), astragalus root extract (380 mg), a fibre blend (4.01 g) and a superfood blend (3.929 g). The blends include tapioca fibre, fructo-oligosaccharides, beta glucan, plant powders and extracts; individual amounts within each blend are not disclosed. Other ingredients include natural flavours, citric acid, DL-malic acid, sea salt and stevia.
