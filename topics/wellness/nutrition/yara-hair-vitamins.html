The linked checkout is a subscription

The observed affiliate checkout for product 599088 charged US$37 initially and US$37 every month, with cancellation available. The sales page also displays three- and six-month subscription options. A “buy once” interface was present, but a one-time order and price were not confirmed. Do not assume the affiliate link opens that alternative; inspect the selected billing cycle immediately before payment.

Warnings and suitability

The label says Contains Soy and includes an iron-related warning about accidental overdose in children. Keep it away from children. Ask a qualified health professional to assess the complete botanical blend, other supplements and medication before use; pregnancy, nursing and medical conditions require particular review. The printed medication-timing wording does not establish a safe interval for every medicine. A biotin amount or product name does not establish that this formula will address the cause of a hair concern.

Returns: read the conditions behind the guarantee

Both policy pages mention 60 days but disagree about procedure. The refund page uses a different brand name and an Aurora return address, and asks for postmark within 60 days of purchase. The shipping policy instead gives an Englewood address and requires returned bottles and bonuses to arrive within 60 days of the original order, with postage paid by the customer. Ask support for written, order-specific instructions before purchasing. Do not choose a return address from these conflicting pages without confirmation.
