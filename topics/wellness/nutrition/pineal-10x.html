Delivery: confirm the actual destination quote

Three- and six-bottle cards advertise free shipping. The supplier policy gives estimated US transit of 5–7 business days and longer international processing/transit. Confirm the destination, separate processing time and actual shipping charge in the final order.

Returns: check the deadline and the procedure

The supplier policy states 365 days from purchase and asks customers to contact support. The sales FAQ describes returning unused product, while the policy also says no explanation is needed after earlier asking for a reason. Confirm required returned items, the correct deadline and who pays postage before ordering.

A label does not validate pineal or manifestation claims

This guide does not establish pineal-gland decalcification, psychic abilities, manifestation outcomes or a disease-treatment effect. Ask a qualified health professional to review the exact iodine-containing botanical formula and current warnings. Supplement marketing is not FDA medicine approval.
