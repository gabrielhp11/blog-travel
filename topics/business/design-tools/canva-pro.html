Who it may suit

Does Canva Pro fit the work you need to do?

Consider it if you regularly create social posts, presentations or promotional materials for a small business. Write down the designs you produce each week. A clear list makes it easier to judge whether the subscription fits your workload and whether the free plan already covers your needs.

Before you choose

Compatibility, access and the terms to confirm

  • Compare Free and Pro using three real tasks you need to complete, rather than a list of features you might never use.
  • Read the content licence for your intended use, especially for client work, merchandise and brand assets.
  • Check your billing currency, VAT treatment, subscription period and renewal amount in the checkout for your country.
  • If you work with colleagues, confirm the appropriate plan, number of users and collaboration features.

Buying Canva Pro: questions to settle first

Does Pro subscription access cover every commercial use?

Do not assume that. Read the content licence for the assets and intended use, particularly client work, merchandise and brand elements. Compare the free plan with your actual tasks before subscribing.

Can I use my designs commercially?

Check the applicable content licence and your intended use before publishing or selling a design.
