Who it may suit

Does Skillshare fit the work you need to do?

It may suit freelancers, content creators and people developing a creative hobby. Before subscribing, choose a small outcome: an illustration, a short film or a photo series. Look for a class that matches your current level and the tools you already have.

Before you choose

Compatibility, access and the terms to confirm

  • Check the language, subtitles, lesson duration and difficulty of the class you want to take.
  • List any additional software, equipment or art supplies needed for the project.
  • Confirm trial eligibility, the full annual charge, billing currency and any tax in your local checkout.
  • Book time to practise and note the trial cancellation deadline if you decide not to continue.
