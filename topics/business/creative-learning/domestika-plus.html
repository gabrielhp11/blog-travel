What the provider offers

A clearer starting point.

The provider describes a rotating course catalogue and credits that can be exchanged for courses. Courses watched through Plus differ from courses acquired with credits. Check access periods, credit expiry and the renewal price for your chosen plan.

Who it may suit

Choose around your own plans.

Consider it if you want to explore different techniques regularly. If you only want one course, compare an individual purchase with the full subscription cost. Separate what you want to sample from the material you need to keep available over time.

Before you choose

Four details worth checking.

  • Check whether the course you want is in the Plus catalogue and how long it remains available.
  • Confirm audio language, English subtitles and materials used by the instructor.
  • Read how many credits you receive, when they expire and what happens if you cancel.
  • Compare the introductory price, renewal total, currency and taxes with buying the course individually.

Is Plus the same as buying a course?

No. The provider distinguishes catalogue access from acquired courses, including credit redemptions.

Will every course stay accessible?

Check the access type of each course; catalogue viewing periods differ from purchases.

How should I compare plans?

Use the courses you intend to finish, credit validity and full renewal cost as your starting point.
