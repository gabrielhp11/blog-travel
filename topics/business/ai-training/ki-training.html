A checklist for your decision

Product details

A free introduction to the provider’s AI-business pitch

The page invites visitors to register for an online training and describes AI as a tool for online income. It gives a personal earnings example and promotes a participant bonus, but does not publish a detailed lesson-by-lesson syllabus or typical results. Treat those figures as the provider’s marketing, not as an expected outcome.

Product fit

What you can use the session to evaluate

If you are comfortable following a German-language presentation, the free session can help you hear how Business Kickstart connects AI tools with online-business activities. It is an introduction to the provider’s approach, not a credential or a promise of revenue.

Before you order

Make the product details work for you

This is an email-led signup for a free session, and the registration page mentions follow-up marketing emails. Check the consent language and any paid products introduced after the training.

Buyer checklist

What to check before you buy

  • Confirm the webinar language, duration, and date before submitting your details.
  • Read the consent text: the seller says it may email tips, strategies, and offers, and that you can withdraw later.
  • Ask what paid course, coaching, or other offer follows the free session, including its price and cancellation terms.
  • Do not use the earnings example as a forecast for your own business. Results depend on skills, time, demand, and execution.
