Before choosing a tool or course

Make your requirements visible.

Who should compare this offer?

Bring a clear goal.

Consider this software if you have a defined business task and can review its output. Write down a small workflow, the accounts it would need and the result you expect to edit. Compare that workflow with a current demonstration before committing to a broad automation package.

Set a realistic budget for the complete project. Include your preparation time, any separate services and the support you will need. Keep a list of the questions the demonstration does not answer.

Common questions

Know what you are choosing.

Does automation guarantee income?

No. We have not verified the seller’s earnings examples, and its own disclaimer says earnings are not guaranteed. Evaluate the software as a tool for a defined task.

Is a bot count a measure of quality?

No. Ask to see your specific workflow, supported connections, limits and editing controls. The claimed number of bots does not establish reliability.

Is access guaranteed forever?

No perpetual availability has been established. The supplier’s terms allow service changes; confirm access duration, backup options and licence restrictions.
