Is this twelve full video lectures or a managed dispute service?

Neither is established by the described package. The supplier lists twelve interactive written modules, worksheets and planning tools. Your business still handles its own records and payment-provider procedures.

Is this a hosted dispute-management service?

No. The supplier describes training and tools in a file that opens in a browser. It does not submit or manage disputes for you. Confirm backups, supported browsers and update arrangements.

Can I share it with my team or resell it?

The public licence is for one paid user or one licensed business, for internal use, and prohibits sharing and resale. Clarify additional access before distributing any material.
