What the provider offers

A clearer starting point.

Who it may suit

Choose around your own plans.

It may fit learners who intend to complete several courses and can set aside regular study time. List three skills relevant to your work, then identify programmes where you can practise them. Compare prerequisites and assignments before committing to a long learning path.

Before you choose

Four details worth checking.

  • Confirm that each chosen programme is included in Plus rather than assuming access to the entire platform.
  • Check teaching language, English subtitles, prerequisites and expected study workload.
  • Compare monthly and annual billing, currency, taxes, trial and refund terms for your country.
  • If you already pay for a separate Specialization, review the subscriptions to avoid overlapping bills.

Frequently asked questions

Coursera Plus: useful answers.

Is every Coursera course included?

No. Check the Plus badge and current inclusion list; exclusions apply.

Is Plus a university degree?

No. The official page excludes degree and MasterTrack programmes from this subscription.

Does a certificate guarantee a job?

No. Use the learning to develop and demonstrate skills; hiring depends on other factors.
