Who should compare this offer?

Bring a clear goal.

This bundle may suit warehouse professionals, analysts and learners who want a structured introduction followed by more technical topics. It includes the shorter SAP EWM Supercharged performance course already covered in a separate PokiSky guide; compare overlap before paying twice.

Set a realistic budget for the complete project. Include your preparation time, any separate services and the support you will need. Keep a list of the questions the demonstration does not answer.

What we have checked

Read the offer with its limits.

The public offer does not establish access to a practice SAP system, an official SAP certification or individual tutoring. A video course alone does not guarantee a job or professional certification.
