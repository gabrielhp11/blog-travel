A platform, plus your work

Is Premium enough for your project?

Start by listing the lessons, media types and access rules you need. If you expect several separate courses, compare another plan before committing. Premium’s published allowance is one course/product.

Use the trial to build a small sample, ask someone to navigate it and note where instructions need improvement. A platform gives your content a place to live; you still prepare the teaching, support your learners and attract your audience.

English software and written help

Check language and business requirements.

The provider offers an English member area and an English help centre. Several help articles are marked as automatically translated. We have not confirmed the language of every included training video.

The provider’s terms address business customers and identify German as the binding contract language. Read those requirements before registering. An English interface does not make every contractual document English.

Coachy advertises EU hosting. Assess your own handling of learner data and the relevant service agreement; hosting location alone does not resolve all your obligations.

Budget for the full year

Annual billing is a yearly payment.

€420 per yearPublished Premium annual price · excludes VAT

The advertised €35 monthly equivalent is billed annually. Monthly billing is listed at €42 before VAT. Compare the commitment and final tax-inclusive total for your business, rather than just the smaller monthly equivalent.

The 30-day trial requires no credit card according to the provider. Its help page says the trial expires automatically; continuing requires choosing a paid plan. A paid subscription renews unless cancelled under the applicable terms.

The pricing FAQ says cancellation takes effect at the end of the billing period, while the terms mention notice of one working day. Check the deadline in your paid agreement and keep a record of cancellation. Back up your course files independently.

Before you begin

Common questions about Coachy.

Is this an English course to take?

It is software for creating your own courses. Its interface and written help are available in English; you supply the lesson content.

Does the trial automatically become paid?

The help centre says it expires automatically. You choose a paid plan if you want to continue. Read the current signup and payment terms.

Is €35 the annual plan’s monthly payment?

It is a monthly equivalent. The published annual charge is €420 before VAT.

Has PokiSky tested a paid account?

No. We checked public information and the referral path to registration. Paid checkout attribution and the affiliate account’s actual commission have not been verified.
