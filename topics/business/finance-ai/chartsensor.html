Before choosing a tool

Does this format suit your learning?

Think about how often you currently study charts, your comfort reading English and the kind of explanation you find useful. If you prefer a fixed syllabus, compare a structured course as well.

For a practical evaluation, bring three questions to the public demo: one definition, one explanation of a chart feature and one follow-up. Consider whether the answers help you identify what you still need to learn.

Check the setup

Clarify compatibility before payment.

The provider describes a built-in chart system. Confirm your device, required browser, supported markets and any usage limits directly with support. We have not tested a paid account or verified integration with another chart platform.
