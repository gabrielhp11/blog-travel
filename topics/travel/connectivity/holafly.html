Who it may suit

Choose around your own plans.

It may interest travellers who prefer comparing connectivity around the length of a trip. If you plan to work remotely, put tethering and local network conditions near the top of your checklist. For European routes, compare every country you will visit and the roaming already available through your own operator.

Before you choose

Four details worth checking.

  • Check your exact device model, eSIM compatibility and carrier-unlocked status.
  • Match the included countries and validity dates to your route; check border crossings separately.
  • Read the package’s fair-use, speed and hotspot conditions, especially for laptop work.
  • Distinguish a one-trip package from a recurring subscription and read renewal and cancellation terms.

Price and conditions

Check the full offer for your country.

Unlimited data should not be interpreted as a guarantee of constant speed or unrestricted tethering on every product. Read the package conditions, final billed amount and refund terms. Compare travel packages separately from monthly or annual subscriptions and include any tax shown at checkout.
