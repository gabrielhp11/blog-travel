A clearer starting point.

Airalo offers local, regional and global eSIM packages with different data allowances and validity periods. Some packages include calls and texts. The provider requires a compatible, carrier-unlocked device; check the requirements and the individual package.

Who it may suit

Choose around your own plans.

Consider it if you need maps, messages and travel apps on a compatible device. Check your recent data usage before choosing an allowance. For a route across Europe, list every country, including stopovers. Also compare the roaming included in your existing mobile plan before buying extra data.

Before you choose

Four details worth checking.

  • Check the exact phone model and regional variant, eSIM support and carrier-unlocked status.
  • Read the country and network list: a Europe package does not automatically include every European destination.
  • Confirm when validity starts, how long it lasts and whether the specific package supports top-ups.
  • If you need regular phone calls or SMS, confirm those features rather than assuming a data package includes them.
