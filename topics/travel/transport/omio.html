Who it may suit

Choose around your own plans.

It may help travellers planning a trip with several stops across Europe. Write down exact departure and arrival locations rather than only city names. Add the cost and time of local transfers so you can compare a train, coach or flight on the same basis.

Before you choose

Four details worth checking.

  • Check the exact station, terminal, airport or port at both ends of the journey.
  • Compare baggage, seats, service fees and the final billed amount in the selected currency.
  • For connections, allow enough time and check whether tickets are separate or part of one booking.
  • Read fare change and cancellation rules, passenger-name requirements and ticket presentation instructions.

Price and conditions

Check the full offer for your country.

The cheapest fare may have more restrictive conditions. Compare the carrier’s direct offer where available using the same luggage and flexibility. Check currency, fees and passenger details. With separate tickets, ask how missed connections are handled before relying on a tight transfer.
