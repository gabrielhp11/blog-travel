Who it may suit

Does Civitatis fit the work you need to do?

It may suit travellers who enjoy historical context and prefer to organise key activities in advance. For excursions beyond a city, compare time on the road with time at the destination. Choose for your interests and your group’s pace rather than trying to visit every highlight in one day.

Before you choose

Compatibility, access and the terms to confirm

  • Confirm English guiding on your selected date; the website language alone is not enough.
  • Check whether transport, entry tickets, meals and equipment are included or optional.
  • Read the meeting point and whether hotel pick-up is available in your area.
  • Review cancellation, accessibility and any minimum-participant requirement on the individual offer.

Frequently asked questions

Buying Civitatis: questions to settle first

Does an English website guarantee an English-speaking guide?

No. Check the guiding language on the exact activity, option and date. Confirm inclusions, meeting point and cancellation conditions for that booking.

Is transport included?

Read the inclusions and meeting point; arrangements vary between activities.

What should I compare between excursions?

Consider travel time, places visited, group pace, language and total cost.
