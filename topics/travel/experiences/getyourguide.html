Who it may suit

Choose around your own plans.

It may help travellers with fixed dates who want to organise one or two highlights per day. Choose your main priority first: a museum, a neighbourhood walk or a day trip. Keep gaps for transport and meals, particularly when a short European break involves several busy attractions.

Before you choose

Four details worth checking.

  • Confirm whether the option includes an entry ticket, a guide, transport or a combination.
  • Check the tour language, duration and meeting point; an English website does not guarantee an English-speaking guide.
  • Read age, accessibility and document requirements for your group.
  • Check the specific activity’s cancellation deadline and change policy, using local times.

Check the full offer for your country.

The final amount depends on date, option and participants. Compare inclusions with the attraction’s own offer where available. Check currency, fees, provider, booking confirmation and voucher instructions. Availability and cancellation must be read on the selected listing.

Frequently asked questions

GetYourGuide: useful answers.

Does every booking include a guide?

No. Check whether the chosen option is a guided experience or an entry ticket.

Can every activity be cancelled?

Read the cancellation terms and deadline of the individual listing before booking.

How can I avoid itinerary clashes?

Allow for travel time and leave a margin between activities, especially when changing neighbourhoods.
