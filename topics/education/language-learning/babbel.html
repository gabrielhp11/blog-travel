Who it may suit

Choose around your own plans.

It may suit learners who prefer regular short sessions and want to fit study around work or travel. Pick a real situation and write down what you would like to say. Supplement app exercises with listening and conversation when possible, especially before moving or travelling abroad.

Before you choose

Four details worth checking.

  • Check which target languages and course levels are available from your base language.
  • Confirm whether the offer covers one language or multiple languages and the devices you want to use.
  • Review the full billed amount, currency, taxes, access period and renewal terms for your country.
  • If buying through an app store, check where to manage cancellation and recurring payments.
