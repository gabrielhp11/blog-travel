Editorial standards & affiliate disclosure

Before buying from Europe

Europe includes different countries, currencies and purchase conditions. This directory groups guides by intended audience; it does not establish that every supplier accepts orders in every country.

  • Language: check the course audio, subtitles, software interface and support language separately from the language of our guide.
  • Currency and total: confirm the checkout currency, applicable taxes, card conversion charges and whether the amount is a one-time payment or a renewing subscription.
  • Country and access: check your billing country, delivery destination, software requirements and licences. UK and EU terms may differ.
  • Purchase conditions: read the seller’s current cancellation, refund, digital-access and support terms before paying. Save the order description and applicable conditions.

Questions before you click through

Short answers from this directory. They do not replace the seller’s live terms.

Do these guides cover every country in Europe?

No. Each guide records an intended audience and purchase context. Confirm your billing country, delivery destination and the seller’s current terms; availability is not established for every European country.

What is the difference between the English and German guides?

English pages are written in English and German pages are written in German. A product’s teaching, interface or support language can still differ from the language of our guide.

Are UK and EU checkout terms the same?

Not necessarily. Currency, tax, delivery, licences and cancellation rules can differ. Read the live checkout for your country before paying.

Does PokiSky sell these products?

No. These are editorial buyer guides. Some links are affiliate links, disclosed on each page and on the disclosure page.
